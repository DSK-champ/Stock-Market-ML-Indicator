Trader's Manual & Guide

1. How to Read in One Second

Look at the FINAL PREDICTION badge. UP (Green) indicates expected expansion upward. DOWN (Red) indicates downward expansion. NO CLEAR SIGNAL (Grey) means market conditions do not provide a statistically verified edge over transaction costs.

2. What Confidence Means

The confidence percentage is the internal model probability $\max(P_{\text{up}}, P_{\text{down}})$. It is not calibrated to represent actual empirical win rates. Do not treat 60% as a 60% certainty.

3. Why NO CLEAR SIGNAL is Normal

Bitcoin markets spend significant time in consolidation where noise dominates signal. Requiring expansion beyond the 23 bps cost floor means the model deliberately filters out ~45% of candles.

4. Prediction Horizon

The prediction horizon is strictly 2 candles (10 minutes). Predictions refer to the price change from the signal candle close to the close of candle $k+2$.

5. Indicative Range Heuristic

The upper and lower dashed lines are visual heuristics calculated from Wilder's 14-period ATR and directional model probabilities. They are indicative bounds, not guaranteed prediction intervals.

6. When NOT to Trust the Signal

  • When the Stale Data banner is visible.
  • Immediately following sudden news spikes or extreme volatility.
  • When status indicates imputed features or insufficient buffer.
  • During the first 5 minutes after a connection drop.

7. Disclaimer

This system is for research, forward-testing, and simulation only. Past performance on historical data is no guarantee of future returns.
