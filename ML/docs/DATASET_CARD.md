## 4. Target Formulation & Economic Dead-Zone

* **Prediction Horizon:** $N=2$ candles forward ($10\text{ minutes}$).
* **Execution Return:** $\text{fwd\_ret\_open} = \frac{\text{Close}_{t+2}}{\text{Open}_{t+1}} - 1$ (conservative entry at next candle open).
* **Economic Friction Floor ($c$):**
  $$c = \text{Taker Fee (0.20\%)} + \text{Slippage (0.02\%)} + \text{Half-Spread (0.01\%)} = 0.2300\% \; (23\text{ bps})$$
* **Labeling Rule (3-Class):**
  $$\text{Target}_{t} = \begin{cases} +1 \text{ (UP)}, & \text{if } \text{fwd\_ret\_open} > +0.0023 \\ -1 \text{ (DOWN)}, & \text{if } \text{fwd\_ret\_open} < -0.0023 \\ 0 \text{ (NEUTRAL)}, & \text{if } |\text{fwd\_ret\_open}| \le 0.0023 \end{cases}$$
* **Class Distribution (Training Split, 220,953 candles):**
  * UP ($+1$): 34.3%
  * DOWN ($-1$): 34.3%
  * NEUTRAL ($0$): 31.4%

## 6. Engineered Feature Taxonomy (66 Causal Features)

1. **Returns & Momentum (12 features):** Log returns over windows $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 6, 12, 24, 72, 288\}$, RSI(14), MACD histogram, Stochastic oscillator (%K, %D).
2. **Realized & Extreme Value Volatility (16 features):** Normalized Average True Range (NATR_14), Parkinson Volatility, Garman-Klass Volatility, Rogers-Satchell Volatility, Bollinger Band Width (%B).
3. **Candle Shape & Microstructure (10 features):** Body-to-range ratio, upper-shadow ratio, lower-shadow ratio, bar polarity.
4. **Order Flow & Taker Imbalance (12 features):** Taker buy ratio ($\frac{\text{taker\_buy\_vol}}{\text{volume}}$), rolling volume z-scores, trade intensity.
5. **Structural Support / Resistance & Dow Theory (10 features):** Swing highs, swing lows, distance to active swing level, breakthrough impulse strength, pullback retracement depth.
6. **Temporal & Calendar Features (6 features):** Sine/cosine cyclic hour of day (24h period), day of week (7d period), weekend liquidity indicator.
